Wiseconomy: The 9-Email Adaptive Funnel

How we recovered cold calculator traffic, resolved Canadian financial jargon barriers, and built an automated lead-nurturing pipeline that turns passive searchers into qualified advisory appointments.

42% Account Sign-Up Lift

TFSA & RRSP account conversions after simplifying jargon.

3.5x Click-to-Lead Ratio

Increase in booked advisor calls from lead-nurture drips.

9 Beats Adaptive Architecture

Segmented by intent, dwell-time triggers & exit paths.

Phase 1 (Emails 01 – 03)

Friction Removal & Intent Categorization

Most quote calculator visitors abandon because financial terms like TFSA, RRSP, and Super Visa deductible rules feel intimidating. Phase 1 demystifies the terminology without sounding like a bank lecture.

Email 01 / Day 0

"Your quote estimate is ready (plus 1 thing most Canadians overlook)"

Delivers the requested estimate immediately while highlighting the exact difference between high-fee retail bank products and Wiseconomy's direct advisory framework.

Trigger: Immediate delivery after calculator submission.
Email 02 / Day 2

"TFSA vs. RRSP: Which one actually saves you more tax this year?"

Breaks down tax-shelter math into simple visual comparisons. Includes click-tracking links to self-segment users by life stage (newcomer, family, retirement planner).

Trigger: Sent 48 hours post-quote. Tracked via link-click intent tags.
Email 03 / Day 4

"Why Canadian banks charge 2.1% management fees (and what to do instead)"

Positions Wiseconomy's transparent fee model against traditional Big 5 bank mutual funds, building algorithmic credibility and regulatory trust.

Trigger: Sent 96 hours post-quote.
Phase 2 (Emails 04 – 06)

Hot-Lead Detection & Dynamic Objection Branching

If a lead opens Emails 1-3 multiple times or clicks on travel insurance links, Phase 2 dynamically branches copy to address specific objections (e.g., policy approval speed, newcomer eligibility).

Email 04 / Day 7

"Super Visa Insurance: The 3 mistakes that delay visitor policy approval"

Targeted specifically at families bringing parents/grandparents to Canada. Outlines mandatory government coverage requirements and instant digital policy issuance.

Trigger: Branching tag = Super Visa / Travel Interest.
Email 05 / Day 10

"What happens if you move or change jobs? (Your policy explained)"

Addresses portability and liquidity objections, reassuring clients that their financial plan stays locked regardless of employment shifts.

Trigger: Sent 10 days post-quote to engaged non-bookers.
Email 06 / Day 14

"15 minutes with a licensed Canadian advisor (No sales pressure)"

The primary conversion gate. Invites leads to a low-friction 15-minute introductory video call to review customized policy options.

Trigger: Sent 14 days post-quote to high-intent leads.
Phase 3 (Emails 07 – 09)

Dead Lead Conversion & Clean Exit Paths

Leads that remain silent after 14 days enter Phase 3. Instead of spamming, we use direct response micro-copy, client proof points, and a clean exit option to prompt action or graceful unsubscribe.

Email 07 / Day 21

"Did you get your TFSA return calculation?"

Ultra-short 3-line text email asking a direct binary question. Re-engages cold inboxes with an 18% reply rate.

Trigger: Unopened / silent lead reset after 21 days.
Email 08 / Day 28

"How Raj & Priya saved $4,200 on emergency travel insurance"

Case study narrative sharing a real Canadian newcomer story, validating emotional trust and personal advisory value.

Trigger: Sent 28 days post-quote.
Email 09 / Day 35

"Permission to close your file?"

The classic breakup email. Respectfully offers to pause communication or move them to the monthly educational newsletter, triggering fear of missing out (FOMO).

Trigger: Final automated touchpoint at 35 days.
